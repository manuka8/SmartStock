# 📦 StockFlow

### Inventory & Stock Management System

**React • Node.js • SQL**

**StockFlow** is a powerful and user-friendly **Inventory and Stock Management System** designed to manage products, sales, deliveries, and stock operations efficiently. The system provides a **modern admin dashboard** with a **well-structured UI/UX**, enabling smooth daily business operations and accurate reporting.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* 📦 Product & Inventory Management
* 📊 Daily & Monthly Sales Reports
* 🚚 Delivery Vehicle Management
* 📥 Goods Receiving (GRN)
* ⚠️ Low Stock Monitoring & Alerts
* 🔁 Reorders Management
* 🧾 Invoice & Sales Management
* 🏢 Agency, Supplier & Customer Management
* 👥 User & Employee Management
* 📈 Dashboard Analytics
* 🔐 Secure Admin & User Authentication
